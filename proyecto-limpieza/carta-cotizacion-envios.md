# Carta de Cotización a Servicios de Envío (Carriers)

**Objetivo:** Obtener tarifas reales de envío desde St. Thomas, ON a principales ciudades canadienses.

---

## Carta para Chit Chats

```
Hello,

My name is Carlos and I am launching an e-commerce business from
St. Thomas, Ontario. I am currently evaluating shipping carriers for
my online store and would like to understand your services and
pricing for my specific needs.

BUSINESS PROFILE

- Location: St. Thomas, Ontario
- Product: Dishwasher detergent in small, lightweight retail boxes
- Sales channel: Shopify e-commerce store
- Expected volume: 20–100 parcels/month initially, scaling to 300+
- Fulfillment: From home/small storage in St. Thomas

PARCEL SPECIFICATIONS

I have two main parcel types:

1. Refill Box (30 doses)
   - Weight: ~400 g
   - Dimensions: ~25 × 18 × 6 cm

2. Refill Box (60 doses)
   - Weight: ~750 g
   - Dimensions: ~25 × 18 × 8 cm

3. Starter Kit (container + 30 doses)
   - Weight: ~1–1.5 kg
   - Dimensions: ~28 × 22 × 12 cm

QUESTIONS

1. What are your current rates for shipping from St. Thomas, ON to
   major Canadian cities (Toronto, Ottawa, Montreal, Vancouver,
   Calgary, Halifax)?

2. Do you have a drop-off or pickup location near St. Thomas?
   If not, what is the closest location?

3. What services would you recommend for small parcels (under 1 kg)
   across Canada?

4. Do you offer integration with Shopify?

5. What are your insurance options for parcels valued at $25–$35 CAD?

6. Are there any minimum volume requirements or monthly fees?

7. What is the typical delivery time from St. Thomas to each province?

8. Do you handle both lettermail-sized and small parcel shipments?

I am comparing carriers and would appreciate a detailed quote or
rate card for my parcel specifications.

Thank you,

Carlos
St. Thomas, Ontario
Canada
```

## Carta para Canada Post (Solutions for Small Business)

```
Hello,

My name is Carlos and I am a small business owner based in
St. Thomas, Ontario. I am launching an e-commerce store and would
like to learn about your Solutions for Small Business program.

BUSINESS PROFILE

- Location: St. Thomas, Ontario
- Product: Dishwasher detergent, lightweight retail boxes
- Expected shipping volume: 20–100 parcels/month initially
- Sales channel: Shopify e-commerce store
- Fulfillment: From home in St. Thomas

PARCEL SPECIFICATIONS

1. Refill Box (30 doses): ~400 g, 25 × 18 × 6 cm
2. Refill Box (60 doses): ~750 g, 25 × 18 × 8 cm
3. Starter Kit: ~1–1.5 kg, 28 × 22 × 12 cm

QUESTIONS

1. What are the current rates and discount tiers for the Solutions
   for Small Business program?

2. What discount levels would I qualify for at 20, 50, and 100
   parcels/month?

3. What services do you recommend for lightweight parcels shipped
   across Canada (Expedited Parcel, Xpresspost, etc.)?

4. What are the delivery timeframes to major Canadian cities?

5. Do you offer integration with Shopify for label printing?

6. What insurance/liability coverage is included?

7. Are there rural or remote surcharges I should account for?

8. Can I schedule regular pickups from St. Thomas?

I am comparing carriers and would appreciate a detailed breakdown
of your rates and program benefits.

Thank you,

Carlos
St. Thomas, Ontario
Canada
```

## Preguntas Clave para Ambos Carriers

| # | Pregunta | Por qué importa |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ¿Cuál es la tarifa real desde **St. Thomas** (no Toronto)? | Las tarifas publicadas son desde Toronto, pueden variar |
| 2 | ¿Hay **pickup** disponible en St. Thomas? | Si no hay, necesitamos llevar lotes a London |
| 3 | ¿Cuál es el **peso dimensional** máximo? | Nuestro producto optimiza dimensiones |
| 4 | ¿Hay **recargos por zonas rurales**? | Algunas partes de Canadá tienen surcharges |
| 5 | ¿Integración con **Shopify**? | Automatización de etiquetas |
| 6 | ¿Seguro incluido o adicional? | Productos de $25–$35 CAD |
| 7 | ¿Tiempo de entrega por **provincia**? | Experiencia del cliente |
