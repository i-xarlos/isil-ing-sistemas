# Carta de Cotización a Fabricantes (RFQ)

**Formato:** Request for Quantity — Private Label  
**Producto:** Detergente automático para lavaplatos en dosis individuales  

---

## Carta en Inglés (para enviar a proveedores canadienses)

```
Hello,

My name is Carlos, and I am currently developing a new Canadian consumer
brand focused on concentrated and refillable household cleaning products.

I am looking for a Canadian manufacturer/contract packager that could help us
develop and manufacture an automatic dishwasher detergent in a dry, single-
dose format for the Canadian market.

At this stage, I am looking to understand your capabilities, minimum order
quantities, and estimated costs before moving forward with product development.

PRODUCT CONCEPT

We are considering:
- Automatic dishwasher detergent powder
- Single-dose sachets, approximately 8–12 g per dose
- 30 doses per retail box for the initial product
- Dry powder formulation suitable for standard residential dishwashers
- Preferably fragrance-free or with a mild fragrance option
- Designed for good performance in hard-water conditions
- Canadian market
- Private-label / custom-brand product

We are also considering a reusable container as part of a Starter Kit, while
future purchases would be sold as refill boxes containing only the individual
doses.

INITIAL QUANTITIES

Could you please provide estimated pricing and MOQ for the following quantities?
- 100 retail boxes (3,000 individual doses)
- 250 retail boxes (7,500 doses)
- 500 retail boxes (15,000 doses)
- 1,000 retail boxes (30,000 doses)

If these quantities are below your normal MOQ, please let us know what your
minimum viable production quantity would be.

PACKAGING

We would like to explore:
1. Individual sachets containing one dose of detergent
2. A retail cardboard box containing 30 sachets
3. Optional reusable container for the Starter Kit

We would appreciate information regarding:
- Available sachet materials
- Moisture-barrier options
- Whether you can manufacture and fill the sachets
- Cardboard box sourcing/printing options
- Packaging setup/tooling costs
- Minimum quantities for custom packaging

PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT AND REGULATORY SUPPORT

Could you also let us know whether you provide:
- Existing dishwasher detergent formulations suitable for private label
- Semi-custom formulation options
- Fully custom formulation/development
- Product samples
- Stability/shelf-life testing
- SDS documentation
- Regulatory/labeling guidance for the Canadian market
- Testing for dishwasher performance
- Support with bilingual English/French labeling

We would prefer to work with a Canadian manufacturer and, where
commercially feasible, Canadian-sourced raw materials and packaging.

COST BREAKDOWN

If possible, please provide an estimated breakdown for:
- Product/formulation cost per kg
- Cost per individual sachet
- Cost per retail box
- Packaging costs
- Setup/tooling fees
- Formulation/development fees
- Testing fees
- Sample costs
- Shipping to St. Thomas, Ontario

We would also appreciate your estimated lead time from approval of the
formulation to finished goods.

At this stage, we are primarily interested in understanding whether this product
can be produced economically at a small initial scale and then scaled as demand
increases.

Please let me know if you need any additional information from me to prepare an
initial quotation.

Thank you,

Carlos
St. Thomas, Ontario
Canada
```

## Preguntas Clave si Responden con Cotización Vaga

1. ¿Cuál es el costo total **puesto en St. Thomas, Ontario**?
2. ¿Cuál es el MOQ real para producto terminado con **nuestro packaging**?
3. ¿Cuánto costaría una **muestra** antes de hacer el primer lote?
4. ¿Pueden producir el **sachet y la caja**, o tendríamos que contratar otro proveedor?

---

## Ideal: Un Solo Proveedor

El proveedor ideal debe entregar:

```
Detergente → Sachet → Caja → Producto terminado
```

No queremos recibir:
- 30,000 sachets sueltos
- 1,000 cajas por separado
- 1,000 contenedores por separado
- Y tener que ensamblarlo todo manualmente

Queremos recibir:
- 1,000 productos terminados listos para vender

---

## Tip: Negociación del Primer Pedido

Si el MOQ es alto (ej. 1,000 cajas), podemos negociar:

| Opción | Estrategia |
|---|---|
| **Producción completa** | 1,000 unidades producidas |
| **Entrega parcial** | 250 unidades a casa, 750 retenidas en bodega del fabricante |
| **3PL parcial** | 250 a casa, 750 a 3PL (solo si es necesario) |

Si el fabricante ofrece **almacenamiento**, eso sería excelente para el MVP.
